---------------- ABOUT THE GAME ----------------
A wealthy business owner hosted a party at their cottage.
During the party, a locker hidden behind a bookshelf in the
Study Room was robbed. Eight people were present that night
and one of them is the CULPRIT. The owner's Personal
Assistant has hired you, a private detective, to solve it.

---------------- OUR MISSION ----------------
1. Visit seven locations. Each gives one letter tile and
   its board position for Word 1.
2. Interview eight people (including the victim). Each gives
   one letter tile and its board position for Word 2.
3. Place every letter on the Scrabble board.
4. When a row is complete, form (type) the word.
5. The two words together point to the culprit's role.
   Accuse that suspect before your credibility runs out!
